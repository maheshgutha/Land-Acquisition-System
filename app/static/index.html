Problem statement

SIH 2026, PS 26016 — Real-Time National Land Acquisition & Management System for End-to-End Digital Monitoring and Decision Support (Ministry of Rural Development, DoLR). Land acquisition for highways, railways, industrial corridors, irrigation and other infrastructure today runs through fragmented, state-specific, largely paper-based processes across Central Ministries, State Governments, District Authorities and Project Implementing Agencies, with limited transparency into where a project actually stands.

What this build does

An end-to-end digital workflow from proposal to possession and R&R, with role-gated approvals at every stage; GIS geo-tagging of parcels with an interactive national map; a live national dashboard covering area, compensation, families, R&R and schedule adherence; mobile-responsive field data capture (GPS and camera); a versioned, tamper-evident document repository; customisable MIS reports; and a trained delay-risk model with per-project explanations. See the full project PDF for a section-by-section walkthrough and an honest breakdown of what is real versus mocked.
